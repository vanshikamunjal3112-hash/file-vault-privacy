File Vault - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

File Vault ("the app") locks your photos and documents on your phone. This policy says what the app does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, send, share or sell any personal data.

  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything off your phone.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no accounts.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The files you choose to lock. They are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and kept in the app's private storage.
  • Your PIN, saved only as a salted hash, and the encryption key, kept in the Android Keystore.
  • While you watch a video or open a document in another app, a temporary unlocked copy is kept in the app's private cache. It is deleted when you leave the video or the vault locks. PDFs and photos are read from memory.
  • A short security log (times of wrong PIN tries and PIN changes, no PIN values). It stays on your phone and you can clear it.
  • If you turn on the optional decoy PIN, a second, separate encrypted vault is stored on your phone. It is never part of backups.
  • If Auto backup is on: one encrypted backup file in Downloads/FileVault/ on your phone. You control this file. The app never uploads it. You can turn Auto backup off in Settings.

Sharing and sensors

  • Share into File Vault: when you share files to the app from another app, it reads only those files, and only after you unlock and confirm.
  • Shake to lock (optional, off by default) reads the phone's motion sensor to notice a hard shake. Motion data is not stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Biometric / fingerprint: only to unlock the app on your phone. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint.
  • The app has no storage, camera, location, contacts or network permission. Files are chosen with the Android system file picker.

Your responsibility and limits

  • If you lose both your PIN and your recovery code, **your files cannot be recovered by anyone**, including the developer.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the vault. Only the backup file can restore it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update and the date above will change.
